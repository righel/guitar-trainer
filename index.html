How to use

  • Sidebar: browse scales, chords, triads and exercises. Folders nest and remember whether they are open; use the filter box to search. Toggle it with ☰ or [.
  • Triads: pick a string set (e.g. strings 2-3-4) to see every closed shape labelled root / 1st / 2nd inversion; playback walks through the shapes up the neck.
  • Theory: the Theory panel explains whatever is selected (formula, steps, usage) plus its section. Click ⓘ next to any sidebar folder to read about that section.
  • Exercises: presets that set up the pattern, tempo subdivision and auto-advance for you — then press Play.
  • Daily routines: pick a time budget (10, 15, 20, 30 minutes or 1 hour) under Daily routines. Each step loads an exercise with a countdown and moves on by itself; Space pauses the clock and the music together. The key, sequence, string set and line style rotate daily. Minutes, streak and the best tempo for each exercise are logged in this browser.
  • Melody over changes: lines that use a scale melodically over a chord progression — chord-tone targets, guide tones, arpeggio → scale, enclosures, motifs and random phrases. Chord tones of the current chord are drawn large; the backing chord is strummed on each change. For random phrases and motifs, press N for a new one.
  • Fretboard: pick key, scale and tuning. Click a note to hear it. Set a Position window (e.g. 5–8) to practise one box.
  • Metronome & progression: press Play. The scale is played in time over the chosen octaves, direction and pattern, and every note lights up on the neck.
  • Random scale machine: each list deals keys in random order without repeats until it is completed. Build your own list with the custom grid, lock it and press Go; save up to 6 selections.
  • Bend trainer: plug the guitar into a line-in or audio interface and press Start listening. Bend the fretted note until the needle sits in the green zone and hold it there; the trace shows how your bend rose, overshot and settled.
  • Auto-advance: while playing, jump to the next random scale every N bars or after each full pass.
